A little clarity

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Can we talk before choosing a package?

Yes. Start with a first conversation to discuss your situation, priorities and timing. You can agree the right scope of support after that conversation.

Can you help if I’m moving with children?

School search and enrollment support are included in the Home & family support. We’ll discuss your children’s needs and your family’s priorities in the consultation.

Is the suggested package a final quote?

No. It is a starting point to help you explore your options. Your final fees, availability and scope will be agreed directly with Christa.

How does the consultation request work?

The form prepares an email in your email app. Review it and send it to Christa. Nothing is submitted to a server by this review website.

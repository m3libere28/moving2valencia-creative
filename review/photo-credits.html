Authored atmosphere & supplied media

The portrait of Christa Brokke was supplied with the Moving2Valencia source. It is used as the founder portrait; generated portraits are not used.

The orange-tree café street and certain coastal and green scenes are generated interpretations, labelled as illustrative in the designs and notes. The cooking scene and architectural miniature are authored 3D scenes. The miniature’s district positions are approximate.

The moving shoreline is Mixkit item 5016, Waves coming to the beach, under the Mixkit Stock Video Free License. Its recording location is not verified as Valencia. Other imported stock photographs were supplied in the M2V-Proposal source, whose README attributes them to Unsplash.

A sense of place.
Clear provenance.

The collection combines actual Valencia photography, generated visual interpretations, authored 3D scenes and recorded coastal atmosphere. Their roles are distinguished below; individual photo and font credits are retained in the credits page.

Actual city photographs

The supplied A New Horizon and Valencia Arrivals sources include licensed photographs of Valencia landmarks, neighbourhood streets, markets, the Turia Gardens and the coast. Their author, original file and Creative Commons license references remain in the pages and shared credits. Responsive WebP derivatives preserve those licenses. The supplied Christa Brokke portrait provides the human introduction.

Generated atmosphere

The warm orange-tree café street and certain coastal/green images are generated interpretations. They are used as illustrative atmosphere, not documentary photographs of a verified property or district. Pan-and-zoom treatments of those stills remain illustrative motion studies.

Native 3D scenes

The animated paella is an authored cooking scene with tossing food, slow rotation, fire, steam and a transparent composition. The Valencia miniature retains its Blender-authored geometry and Unreal-rendered tour, with architectural detail, a travelling light, tracked markers and shoreline foam. It is an art-directed miniature with approximate neighbourhood positions, not a navigation or property map.

Recorded waves

Immersive Valencia and the Motion opening use Mixkit’s Waves coming to the beach, item 5016, under the Mixkit Stock Video Free License. The location is not verified as Valencia. Its role is coastal atmosphere. The failed photograph-displacement effect is removed.

Lighter web pages, complete downloads

Web editions share compressed assets, local fonts and local animation scripts. Azahar’s embedded photographs are extracted into cacheable files, with smaller phone sources and deferred images. Video controls, offscreen pausing, background-tab pausing and reduced-motion alternatives are preserved. Each standalone HTML embeds the images and animation needed for its review; external credit and business links remain available when online.

An honest next step

Enquiry forms prepare a reviewable email draft. They do not represent an automatically sent request or a confirmed appointment. Suggested times, support scopes and neighbourhood preferences start a conversation; fees and availability are agreed with Christa. The user’s original supplied archives remain unchanged.

Legal & Administrative Formalities in Valencia

Practical help with registrations, appointments and paperwork.

A successful and stress-free relocation depends greatly on getting the important legal and administrative formalities right and taking care of them on time.

Yet this is often the part that people find most frustrating. It requires a good understanding of the local procedures, the relevant authorities and the paperwork involved, not to mention dealing with the language and cultural barriers.

At Moving2Valencia, we can guide you through and assist you with many of these formalities. You can choose the specific services you need, or include them in one of our tailored relocation packages.

Some procedures require more specialised legal or professional advice. In those cases, we will be happy to connect you with reliable local and international experts from our network.

Visa and immigration lawyers

For visa applications, such as the Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV), Digital Nomad Visa (DNV) and other types of residence permits, we work with reliable immigration lawyers who specialise in these procedures. We don't handle visa applications ourselves, but we can connect you directly with the right professionals to guide you through the legal process.

Tax advice before moving to Spain

Tax matters are also very important to consider before moving to Spain. Spain has its own tax rules and obligations, which may be quite different from those in your home country. On top of that, each autonomous community has certain regional tax regulations, and the rules in the Comunidad Valenciana can differ from those in other parts of Spain. Your tax situation will depend on your personal circumstances and where you settle, so we strongly recommend speaking with a qualified tax specialist before making the move. We can connect you with reliable tax advisors who have experience assisting international clients relocating to the Valencia region.

Our mission is to help you find the right support and solutions for your particular needs and goals, so that your relocation to Valencia can be as smooth and stress-free as possible.

Our practical assistance

  • NIE (Foreigner Identification Number) – assistance with the application process.
  • EU Residence Certificate (Certificado de Registro de Ciudadano de la Unión).
  • TIE Residence Card (Tarjeta de Identidad de Extranjero) – practical assistance for non-EU citizens who have received approval for their residence permit.
  • Social Security Number – when starting employment in Spain or registering as self-employed (autónomo).
  • Opening a Spanish bank account.
  • SIP Health Card (Tarjeta Sanitaria) – application for access to the Spanish public healthcare system, when eligible.
  • Insurance – assistance with arranging private health, home, car and other insurance policies.
  • Town Hall Registration (Empadronamiento).
  • EU car registration – we connect you with reliable professionals who specialise in this process.
  • Transfer of your driving licence.
  • Organising your removal to Spain.
  • Customs clearance.
  • Departure services – practical assistance with the arrangements involved when leaving Spain.

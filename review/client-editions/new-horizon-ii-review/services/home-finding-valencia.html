Home Finding in Valencia

Independent help renting or buying, from the shortlist to moving day.

At Moving2Valencia, we are independent relocation specialists helping you find your ideal home in Valencia, whether you are looking to buy or rent. Unlike real estate agents, we don't have our own property listings. Our focus is entirely on your needs and finding a home that suits you.

We start discussing your requirements while you are still in your home country. Based on your needs and preferences, we search for a selection of suitable properties through our network of reliable local real estate agents and other property professionals. We hope that one of them will be your perfect home!

We organise all the property viewings around your personal schedule. Once you arrive in Valencia, we accompany you to the selected properties and introduce you to the local areas, helping you get a better feel for what it would be like to live there.

Once you have found the home you are looking for, we assist you with many of the formalities involved, making your move as smooth and easy as possible.

Our independence, experience and local knowledge help you avoid common pitfalls, save valuable time and money, and make informed decisions throughout the process.

We don't work for landlords or real estate agents. We work exclusively for you and in your best interests.

What’s included in Home Finding

  • Making a pre-selection of properties based on your requirements and preferences.
  • Sending you links and details of selected properties for you to review.
  • Helping you prepare a strong rental profile to present to real estate agents and landlords.
  • Scheduling property viewings with owners and real estate agents.
  • Accompanying you to up to 8 property viewings.
  • Reviewing and translating the rental agreement and assisting with negotiating the rental conditions with the landlord.
  • Connecting you with reliable property lawyers when purchasing a property.
  • Connecting you with a reliable mortgage broker if you need financing.
  • Arranging the connection of electricity, water, gas, mobile phone, fibre internet and TV services.
  • Checking the property together with you after moving in and helping you identify any repairs or issues to report to the landlord within the first 15 days.
  • Arranging home insurance.
  • Settling-in assistance on moving day (included in the family relocation packages only).

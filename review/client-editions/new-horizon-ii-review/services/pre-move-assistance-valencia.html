Pre-Move Assistance in Valencia

Get to know the city and surrounding areas before you move.

Relocating to a new home and community is an important step for everyone, and having the right information beforehand is key to making the process as easy and smooth as possible.

You probably have many questions, and that's great! But even if you're not sure what to ask, we'll help make sure you get all the important information and insights you need.

Our Pre-Move Assistance service is included in most of our packages and provides you with a quick yet comprehensive introduction to Valencia and the relocation process.

We'll share our extensive local knowledge about neighbourhoods and residential areas, public transport, schools and healthcare options. We'll also give you recommendations about cultural attractions, restaurants, shopping areas, local businesses, places to enjoy nature and outdoor activities, and so much more.

We help you shortcut the process, saving you valuable time and effort compared to doing all the research yourself. This allows you to narrow down your options, make better-informed decisions and get a clearer picture of what living in Valencia could look like in a short time.

And it's also a truly enjoyable way to get to know Valencia and its beautiful surroundings!

Orientation tours in Valencia

You can also book our Pre-Move Assistance or an Orientation Tour as a stand-alone service, even if you're not planning to move to Valencia just yet. It's a great way to get to know the city and the surrounding areas, explore your options and start preparing for a future move.

What’s included

  • Welcome & Introduction Meeting (2 hours)
  • Orientation Tour (4 hours) through Valencia city and/or the surrounding region.

We'll shower you with useful information, practical tips and local insights covering the key aspects of your relocation to Valencia.

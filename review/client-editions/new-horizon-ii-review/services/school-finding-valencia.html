School Finding in Valencia

Compare school options and get help with visits and enrolment.

Ensuring your children are settled into new schools that best fit their needs and goals, is a truly important step to securing their continued ease and effectiveness. Not to mention, your own peace of mind.

We know most kids understandably struggle with these transitions, as a new school implies not just a proper education system, but also their new community of friends and educators.

At Moving2Valencia, we're committed to making this process as smooth and positive as possible for the whole family. Having experienced this ourselves, we understand the challenges and emotions that a school change can bring, both for children and their parents.

But there are many great school options in Valencia and the surrounding region! There are also quite a few important things to consider, and we're here to help you navigate them.

Whether you're considering public, semi-private (concertado) or private international schools, or have specific requirements regarding special educational needs, languages, subjects or extracurricular activities, we'll help you explore the options and find a school that best fits your family's needs and preferences.

Understanding the school enrolment process

It's important to know that the enrolment process is quite different for public and semi-private (concertado) schools compared to private and international schools. For public and concertado schools, if you're applying based on where you live, it's worth arranging your town hall registration (empadronamiento) early. The official admission criteria can also take a parent or guardian’s work address into account. School places are allocated according to the official admission criteria and application calendar.

For private and international schools, you can usually start contacting schools, arranging visits and exploring enrolment options while you're still in your home country, well before you actually move to Valencia.

We'll help you understand the different procedures and timing, so you know what to expect and when to take the necessary steps.

And once you've made your choice, we'll also assist you with the enrolment process, arranging meetings with teachers and taking care of the necessary paperwork.

Our school-finding assistance

  • Assistance with registration with the Valencian education authorities, where applicable.
  • School pre-selection based on your family's needs and preferences.
  • Arranging and accompanying you on school visits.
  • Assistance with arranging meetings and interviews with teachers.
  • Help with completing the enrolment forms and paperwork.

And if you need additional support or have specific questions, just ask us. We'll be happy to see how we can help!

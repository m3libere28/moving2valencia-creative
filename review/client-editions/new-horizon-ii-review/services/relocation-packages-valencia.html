Relocation Packages in Valencia

Compare what’s included and find the right level of support.

Over the years, we have developed a series of standard relocation packages based on the most common needs and requests of our clients.

However, we also understand that every individual and family is unique. That's why we are happy to offer a Tailor-Made Package, designed around your particular needs, preferences and objectives.

Basic Home Finding Package

  • Home Finding: Assistance with finding your new home.
  • Formalities: Assistance with opening a Spanish bank account, setting up a mobile phone line and arranging fibre internet installation.
  • Settling In: 1 month of online support, including answering any questions clients may have and providing information about local leisure activities, facilities, and language school options.

Basic Relocation Package

  • Pre-Move Assistance
  • Home Finding: Assistance with finding your new home.
  • Formalities: Assistance with opening a Spanish bank account, setting up a mobile phone line and arranging fibre internet installation, NIE or TIE assistance, and arranging home and/or health insurance.
  • Town Hall Registration: Assistance with registering at your local town hall (empadronamiento).
  • Settling In: 2 months of online support, including answering any questions clients may have and providing information about local leisure activities, facilities, and language school options.

Family Relocation Package

  • Pre-Move Assistance
  • Home Finding: Assistance with finding your new home.
  • Formalities: Assistance with opening a Spanish bank account, setting up a mobile phone line and arranging fibre internet installation, NIE or TIE assistance, and arranging home and/or health insurance.
  • Town Hall Registration: Assistance with registering at your local town hall (empadronamiento).
  • Settling In: School search assistance, 2 months of online support, including answering any questions clients may have and providing information about local leisure activities, facilities, and language school options.

Tailor-Made Relocation Package

Would you prefer to put together your own package? We'll send you a form where you can select the services you need. Based on your choices and specific requirements, we'll prepare a personalised proposal for you.

Visa and residence permit applications

Please note that Moving2Valencia does not handle visa or residence permit applications, including the Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV), Digital Nomad Visa (DNV) or other visa procedures. We work with reliable immigration lawyers who specialise in these applications and can connect you directly with them.

Our NIE and TIE assistance covers practical help with the relevant administrative procedures. TIE assistance is available once your visa or residence permit has been approved.

What we do

Everything between “we’re moving” and “we live here.”

01Pre-move planning+

A timeline built around your family, visa type, budget and deadline. Appointments and requirements are discussed around your circumstances.

  • Document checklist — apostilles, sworn translations
  • Phase-by-phase plan with milestones
  • Cost-of-living and budget breakdown
02Legal & formalities+

NIE appointments vanish in minutes. Empadronamiento wants exact documents. Banks want your NIE first. We know the order and the workarounds.

  • NIE / TIE / EU certificate appointments
  • Empadronamiento
  • Bank account and health insurance setup
03Home finding+

We match neighbourhoods to people, then prepare your rental dossier so you can say yes the moment the right flat appears.

  • Curated viewings, in person or by video
  • Lease review (LAU)
  • Utilities and internet transfer
04School search+

Public, concertado, British, American, IB. We visit with you and meet the people who will teach your children.

05Intercultural coaching+

Dinner at ten, two kisses, Fallas in March, Valencian next to Spanish. Understanding the rhythm is what turns a move into a life.

06Ongoing support+

A confusing Hacienda letter, a TIE renewal, a lease question. The relationship doesn’t end at the keys.

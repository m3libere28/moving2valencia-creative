Recent award research

The research focuses on documented winners within the last three years. Awards are reference points; the decisions below are our interpretation of the source material, not claims that these concepts match an award-winning site’s performance.

  • Kane — 2025 Best of Show Mobile Website. The award organizer verifies the recognition. Cuker’s first-party case study describes clearer brand storytelling, an improved shopping journey and conversion-focused UX. Our application: give each section a purpose, explain the service before asking for commitment, and keep a clear primary action.
  • Freshy — 2025 Outstanding Website and 2026 Communicator Award recognition. The agency’s case study describes a bold creative identity coupled with improved discovery and shopping. Our application: the playful direction uses warm color blocks, strong type and memorable shapes while keeping the same practical relocation flow.
  • The Best You by Klook — Awwwards Site of the Day and Developer Award, 11 June 2025. The official listing provides a recent travel-sector reference. Our application: a destination-led opening and a progression from aspiration to concrete next steps. The project detail page could not be retrieved, so this is a thematic reference rather than a claimed detailed interaction audit.
  • Trawelt — Awwwards Site of the Day and Developer Award, 8 June 2025. The official listing supplies a second travel-sector reference. Our application: neighborhood exploration helps the visitor connect the destination with their own routine. We have not benchmarked the award winner.

Implementation choices

  • Buildless HTML, CSS and deferred vanilla JavaScript. No framework hydration or third-party runtime.
  • Two WebP image sizes, responsive source selection, eager hero imagery and lazy secondary images. Shared assets are reusable across the collection.
  • System fonts avoid additional font downloads and font-swap layout shifts.
  • Native disclosure elements for services and FAQs; native selects, checkboxes and dialog controls.
  • Keyboard-operable neighborhood tabs, visible focus, associated labels, semantic landmarks, a skip link and reduced-motion support.
  • Mobile navigation and a bottom action with safe-area padding. Normal scrolling; content is immediately visible.
  • Checklist progress persists locally. Consultation requests prepare an email and never claim that a message has already been sent.

Review status

Browser checks cover 320px and 390px phone viewports and 1280px desktop layouts, image loading, navigation, disclosures, neighborhood selection, keyboard tab movement, package prices, checklist persistence and dialog opening and dismissal. JavaScript syntax and local asset/link checks also pass.

These checks are not a Lighthouse report or a guarantee of Core Web Vitals. Performance in real conditions requires measurement on the chosen production hosting and representative devices. Full screen-reader and physical-device testing remain before public launch.

Before public launch

Confirm inherited package pricing and service commitments, approve the final copy and imagery, and choose one direction. The founder’s background, contact address, prices and package details were taken from the existing local website. Neighborhood copy is editorial starting-point content, not a live property listing. No testimonials or customer counts have been invented.

The collection is for private design review. The original website files have been preserved.

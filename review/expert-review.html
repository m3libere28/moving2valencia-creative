Review standard

The official Webby website and mobile criteria assess content, navigation, visual design, functionality, interactivity, innovation and the overall experience. The Awwwards mobile excellence guidelines emphasize usability and performance. The implication for these concepts: one memorable visual idea should lead into an easy, useful relocation decision, with responsive content and truthful conversion paths.

01 — Casa Editorial

Keep: warm paper, restrained serif typography, portrait-like architectural crop, human founder introduction.

Priority: the evocative hero previously required too much reading before explaining what support was available. The shared structure made the direction feel like a color variation.

Implemented: explicit home/family/practical capability line; an editorial chapter card with a vertical accent rule and direct service entry; refined architectural crop and editorial headline width. Actual image dimensions replace guessed aspect metadata. Service inquiries carry their topic into the contact form.

Further production craft: commission genuine lived-in Valencia photography and a verified client story, with consent. A original photo essay would give the editorial route more distinctive depth than additional animation.

02 — Blue Horizon

Keep: ocean-blue palette, panoramic opening, restrained gold and photographic immersion.

Priority: the photograph and aspiration should support a clear service proposition. Mobile text could compete with bright image regions, and a decorative seal occupied useful space.

Implemented: directional plus bottom image gradient for readable depth, clearer relocation capability line, distinct cinematic chapter transition into neighborhood exploration, simplified mobile hero and removed mobile seal. Headline remains immediately visible; content is usable without animation.

Further production craft: capture authentic coastal footage and local sound with a user-controlled mute state if audio is introduced. Current imagery remains illustrative where noted in media provenance.

03 — Hola, Valencia

Keep: the accepted rotating/tossing paella, flames and steam, friendly peach palette and quiet studio background. Do not restore the rejected sun-and-tile decoration.

Priority: a food animation can be memorable while leaving visitors unclear whether the business is a restaurant. Useful service language must sit beside it, rather than expanding decorative motion.

Implemented: explicit relocation capabilities, a warm chapter card saying what help accompanies the welcome, stronger narrow-phone headline sizing and readable motion-control captions. No changes to the accepted pan/background. The expensive cooking runtime is now loaded only on this concept.

Further production craft: native cookware/material realism can still improve with a dedicated render pass. Avoid increasing browser particles or file sizes just to imply sophistication. The existing pause/reduced-motion lifecycle should remain.

04 — The Valencia Plan

Keep: calm green, clear packages, checklist and practical sequencing.

Priority: the page should operate as a helpful planning tool, not only look more functional than its siblings. The former hero opened contact before letting a visitor make useful decisions.

Implemented: primary hero link goes straight to the starting-plan tool; purpose-specific chapter entry; downloadable plain-text plan includes selected household, package, scope and checked steps. A desktop sticky form supports easy comparison; mobile stays in normal flow. Pricing is explicitly a starting scope, not a confirmed quote.

Further production craft: verified availability and approved business pricing can support a real booking integration. Do not simulate confirmation before a real backend exists.

05 — Editorial Journey (independent cross-review)

Keep: journey illustration, expressive typography, local narrative and language choice. It has the strongest sense of an authored story among the imported proposals.

Priority: source copy claims 500+ families, fixed six-week progress and specific arrival outcomes without supporting evidence. Scheduling language implies time availability while the actual interaction prepares an email. Credibility matters as much as visual polish.

Recommendation sent to proposal agent/root: replace unsupported numerical claims, frame journey milestones as illustrative and contingent, describe contact as a request and apply the wording consistently to translations. Preserve horizontal journey affordances but make keyboard and narrow-phone use clear. Ensure state changes are announced and reduced motion leaves all content visible.

06 — Valencia Bento (independent cross-review)

Keep: lively modular hierarchy, useful neighborhood matcher and clear visual rhythm. A guided decision is stronger marketing than generic decoration.

Priority: dense cards and very large text must preserve a clear path on narrow phones; every matcher step needs a recoverable previous state and a useful final inquiry. Testimonials and decorative portraits need verified identity/permission before launch.

Recommendation: expose results without unsupported certainty, summarize selected preferences in the contact path, keep touch targets generous and prevent sticky controls from obscuring cards. Treat personal preferences as a starting conversation rather than a definitive neighborhood recommendation.

07 — Immersive Valencia (independent cross-review)

Keep: water treatment, coastal visual, restrained floating navigation and scenic storytelling. The direction provides the collection's most immersive first impression.

Priority: the WebGL scene must remain enhancement over a usable static image. Large images, hero letter animations and language-driven innerHTML updates can create poor mobile or reduced-motion behavior. Pricing inputs should announce their calculated result and explain scope.

Recommendation: maintain reliable image fallback, pause expensive work offscreen, keep the whole title translated, use honest quote language, make dialog focus/escape behavior dependable and retain a visible next action after cinematic content. Motion should express arrival, not postpone reading.

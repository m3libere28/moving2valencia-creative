Native 3D Valencia atlas

The miniature geometry and a reference animation were authored in Blender 5.2.1. The website’s final 48-second clip was rendered in Unreal Engine 5.8 through Movie Render Queue: 1,152 frames at 24fps and 1440 × 1080. A native animated travel light visits El Carmen, Ruzafa, El Cabanyal and Benimaclet. The web photo/story card follows each arrival, and visitors can select, pause, replay or save a stop. The compressed MP4 is embedded in the standalone HTML. A slow full camera orbit reveals tiled coastal houses, clay roofs, balconies and church towers, while animated physical foam ribbons move toward the shore. Clickable markers follow the model as it turns.

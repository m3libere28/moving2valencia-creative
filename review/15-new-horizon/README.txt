A New Horizon — fixed source edition

Open index.html in a modern browser, or serve this folder over HTTP.
Keep img/ and vendor/ beside index.html. A-New-Horizon.html in the review
collection is the single-file version with images and animation embedded.

Repairs: valid HTML document with English language and viewport metadata;
local GSAP and ScrollTrigger scripts; readable, unpinned phone hero;
mobile navigation and wrapping lifestyle tabs; motion rebuild and anchor
navigation fixes; whitespace validation and clipboard selection fallback.
Motion respects the system preference and has a user-controlled toggle.

Google Fonts is optional and needs a connection; system font fallbacks
remain available. The form prepares an email draft and does not send it.
Business details marked with a dagger still require owner confirmation.

NOTES.md is preserved from the original source package. Its historical
test results and storyboard describe the supplied design, not this build.
Image credits and license links are retained in the page footer.

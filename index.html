The Journey
2004

The leap across Europe

After 30 years coordinating opera productions in stadiums across the Netherlands, France, Belgium, and Germany, Christa made the decision that would change everything.

She packed her family's life into boxes and drove south — toward a city she'd only visited twice, but felt destined to call home.

2008

From struggle to purpose

Those first years weren't easy. Spanish bureaucracy nearly broke her. NIE appointments, empadronamiento queues, incomprehensible official letters. But she discovered something profound.

The pain she endured could become the gift she gave others. Moving2Valencia was born from a simple belief: no one should navigate this alone.

2024

500 families, one mission

From San Francisco to Singapore, Berlin to Buenos Aires — families arrive anxious and leave with keys in hand, schools enrolled, and a neighbourhood that already feels like home.

Every relocation is a story. Christa makes sure it's a good one.

02 Legal & Formalities +

We navigate the maze so you don't

Spanish bureaucracy is legendary. NIE appointments vanish within minutes of release. Empadronamiento requires exact documents. Bank accounts need your NIE first. We know every step, every deadline, every workaround — built over 16 years.

  • NIE / TIE / EU Certificate appointment booking
  • Empadronamiento (municipal registration)
  • Spanish bank account opening (BBVA, CaixaBank)
  • Private health insurance setup (Adeslas, Sanitas)
Book Free Consultation →
03 Home Finding +

The home that fits your life

We don't just show apartments — we match neighbourhoods to personalities. Ruzafa for creatives and expats, El Carmen for history lovers, El Cabanyal for beach families, Benimaclet for the community-minded. We prepare your full rental dossier so you can say yes the moment the right flat appears.

  • Neighbourhood personality matching
  • Curated viewings (in-person or virtual)
  • Lease review and negotiation (LAU law)
  • Utility transfer and internet setup

Meet Your Guide

From opera stadiums to your front door

Before Valencia, I spent 30 years as a master coordinator for large-scale opera productions across Europe — managing hundreds of artists, impossible deadlines, and thousands of moving parts in stadiums across the Netherlands, France, Belgium, and Germany.

In 2004, I moved my family to Valencia with two suitcases and a lot of courage. The bureaucracy nearly broke me. The language confused me. But Valencia welcomed me completely — and I fell in love with a city unlike any other in Europe.

Moving2Valencia was born in 2008 from a simple realisation: what I had learned the hard way, I could make effortless for others. Every family I help is a gift I give to the city that gave me everything.
